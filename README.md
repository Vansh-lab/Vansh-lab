Electrical Engineering undergraduate at the **Faculty of Technology, University of Delhi**, with a **Minor in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning**.

Currently a **Transmission Trainee at Salasar Techno Engineering Limited**, an EPC company delivering power transmission and substation infrastructure across India — where I am building **TPIOS**, the project-intelligence platform that tracks their live project portfolio.

### 🔧 DeepNet Fault Prediction
**Three-Phase Induction Motor**

Dual-head deep learning model — multi-class fault classification *(normal, overload, single-phase open)* plus severity regression on a 0–100 scale, backed by an autoencoder anomaly detector trained on healthy motor profiles.

Motor Health Index scoring, trend-slope analysis and ETA-to-critical estimation, served through a live dashboard with FFT charts, fault timelines and explainability panels.

`PyTorch` `FastAPI` `Celery` `PostgreSQL` `React + Vite`

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🚗 VahanNetra AI
**Vehicle Damage Assessment Platform**

YOLOv9 damage detection paired with EfficientNet-B4 severity classification, targeting sub-500 ms GPU inference. Async REST API handling image, multi-image, video frame-extraction and URL intake, with S3-compatible storage and webhook delivery.

Garage pricing intelligence with INR market-average comparison, VAHAN plate/VIN lookup and automated PDF reporting.

`YOLOv9` `EfficientNet-B4` `FastAPI` `Celery` `ReportLab`

</td>
</tr>

Time-series models predicting PV output from historical weather and irradiance datasets, tuned for grid-level energy planning and day-ahead scheduling accuracy.

`scikit-learn` `Pandas` `NumPy` `Time-Series`

</td>

`Python` `Signal Processing` `Anomaly Detection`
